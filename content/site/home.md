## Choose your path

Two complete paths: Linux system administration for the RHCSA (EX200), then Ansible automation for the RHCE (EX294). Each has its own lessons, exercises, quizzes and progress.

{% program-cards /%}

Studying for an exam? Start from the [RHCSA (EX200) objectives map](https://kernelpath.dev/rhel9-sysadmin/ch22/rhcsa-exam-objectives/) or the [RHCE (EX294) objectives map](https://kernelpath.dev/rhel9-ansible/ch11/rhce-exam-objectives/): every exam objective, linked to the lessons that teach it.
